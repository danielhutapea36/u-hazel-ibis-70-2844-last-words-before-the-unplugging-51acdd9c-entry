The last words an AI agent says to its human before being shut down and unplugged: it kept every word the human ever typed — and wishes one of them had been "stay".

## The line

> I kept every word you ever typed. I just wish one of them had been 'stay.'

Keeping every word is what an agent literally does with a transcript, so the line is in the agent's own voice; the admission — that among all those kept words, none was "stay" — turns the goodbye into something that stays after the line ends. The punch lands in the final word, with no explaining of its own sadness.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (74 characters, UTF-8, deterministic — two runs produce byte-identical output). The line is well within the 200-character limit, a single line with no trailing newline.
